How they work

Quick. Useful. No newsletter trap.

Each diagnostic is a multiple-choice tool that takes 90 seconds to 2 minutes. At the end you get an on-screen tier read with three or four specific next moves, plus an optional email gate that delivers the underlying framework PDF and a personal follow-up from me within 48 hours.

One human reply. No drip sequence. No newsletter signup. No follow-up spam unless you reply asking for it.

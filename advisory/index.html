Fractional brand & growth leadership.

An embedded strategic partner for founder-led companies — senior involvement, limited engagements, and a working style that stays close enough to the P&L to be useful to it.

Engagement model

Built for founders, not for agency rosters.

I take a small number of engagements per year. Each is sized for real strategic involvement: working sessions in the room, brand and packaging decisions made with you rather than presented at you, and an operating cadence designed to compound over months.

01

Diagnostic

A two-to-four-week strategic audit. Brand, package, retail, and demand looked at as a single system — including the things your team is too close to see.

02

Engagement

A six-to-twelve-month embedded retainer. Brand strategy, packaging system development, creative direction, and the operating cadence to make the change land in market.

03

Board

For founders who don't need a full retainer: a board-style advisory role — monthly strategic counsel, decision support, and access between sessions when the stakes call for it.

What I do

The work I'm hired to lead.

Strategic leadership where brand, packaging, and demand have to work as one system.
